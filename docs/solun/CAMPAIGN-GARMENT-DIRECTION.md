# SOLUN Campaign Garment Direction

**Date:** 2026-11-27  
**Based on:** SWIMWEAR-DESIGN-RESEARCH.md findings  
**Purpose:** Original garment concepts for each SOLUN collection — commercially wearable, physically plausible, architecturally intentional

---

## Design Philosophy Applied

Each garment emerges from the collection's material question:

- **MONOLITH:** *How much can be removed while retaining structure?* → Single continuous loop, zero hardware, bonded seams
- **LUMEN:** *What happens when light becomes the material?* → Double-layer refractive mesh, body as optical system
- **STRATA:** *How does geological logic translate to garment?* → Three tonal bands, ribbed weights, hand-oxidized brass at stress points

**Palette:** Obsidian (#0d0c0b), Charcoal (#1a1a1a), Basalt (#2d2d2d)

## STRATA COLLECTION — Sedimentary / Textural

**Material:** Three-weight ECONYL® ribbed fabric (light 180g / medium 220g / heavy 280g), hand-oxidized brass hardware  
**Palette:** Terracotta (#c45a3a), Sienna (#a0522d), Ochre (#cc7722), Sandstone (#d4b896)  
**Construction Philosophy:** Geological logic. Three tonal bands fold into each other. Hardware marks stress points as geological folding.

## Production Notes (Not Fit-Tested Claims)

> **Disclaimer:** These are design concepts for campaign imagery. No physical prototypes exist. No fit testing has been conducted. Construction details are based on researched industry standards, not validated production.

**Critical Construction Questions for Future Development:**
1. Monolith loop tension calibration per size — requires wear testing
2. Lumen mesh layer sizing — 5% oversizing hypothesis needs validation
3. Strata band weight grading — proportional vs. absolute scaling
4. Brass hardware oxidation consistency — hand process variability
5. Ultrasonic bond durability on ECONYL — 100+ chlorine hours target
