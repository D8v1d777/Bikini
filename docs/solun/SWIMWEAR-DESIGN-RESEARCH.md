## Key Findings by Category

### 1. Silhouette & Garment Proportions

| Brand | Signature Silhouette | Key Proportion Notes |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|
| ERES | High-leg, square-neck, structured one-pieces | Emphasises waist-to-hip ratio; architectural necklines |
| Hunza G | Square-neck, thick straps, high-cut leg | Crinkle fabric creates volume; forgiving fit across sizes |
| Matteau | Clean lines, strapless/bandeau, square neck | "The Square" — geometric neckline as architectural element |
| Monday | Adjustable triangle, bandeau, high-waist bottoms | Size-inclusive grading; adjustable everything |
| Skims | Seamless, second-skin, sculpting | Shapewear logic applied to swim: compression zones |
| Oséree | Cut-out, chain-detail, metallic | Jewelry-as-hardware; decorative but structural chains |
| Solid & Striped | Classic triangle, scoop neck, moderate coverage | Timeless proportions; minimal hardware |

**Verified Detail:** ERES uses a proprietary "Peau Douce" fabric with 22% elastane for recovery. Hunza G's crinkle is a textured nylon-elastane knit that stretches 4-way without distortion. Matteau's strapless styles use internal boning and silicone gripper elastic.

### 2. Cup Construction & Bust Support

**Structured Cup Approaches:**
- **ERES:** Pre-formed foam cups with underwire channelling; side boning for lateral support
- **Matteau:** Soft cups with internal powernet lining; architectural seaming replaces foam
- **Skims:** Compression-moulded cups; no underwire — support via fabric tension
- **Monday:** Removable soft cups; adjustable strap placement for cup position

**Support Strategies Observed:**
1. **Underwire + foam** (ERES) — maximum structure, traditional
2. **Internal powernet + strategic seaming** (Matteau) — architectural, hardware-free
3. **Compression zones** (Skims) — shapewear-derived, seamless
4. **Adjustable geometry** (Monday) — user-customisable fit

**Key Insight:** The most architectural approaches (Matteau, ERES structured) use **seam placement as structural engineering** — princess seams, side-panel seams, and under-bust bands carry load.

### 3. Strap Engineering & Adjustability

| Brand | Strap System | Adjustability |
|-------|-------------|---------------|
| ERES | Fixed-width, often architectural arrangement | Limited — designed for specific proportions |
| Hunza G | Thick crinkle straps, fixed length | None — fabric stretch accommodates |
| Matteau | Thin rouleau straps, convertible | Multi-way (strapless, halter, cross-back) |
| Monday | Thin adjustable with slider hardware | Full — all straps adjustable |
| Skims | Integrated into garment (razorback, tank) | None — stretch fit |
| Oséree | Chain + fabric hybrid | Chain links as adjustment |
| Solid & Striped | Classic adjustable with metal slides | Standard adjustment |

**Verified Detail:** Matteau's convertible straps use hidden buttonholes and rouleau loops — no visible hardware when worn strapless. Monday uses plastic slider hardware for chlorine resistance. Oséree's chain straps are decorative but functional — the chain links allow micro-adjustment.

### 4. Back Construction

**Common Patterns:**
- **Classic tie-back** (ERES, Solid & Striped) — adjustable but can dig
- **Hook-and-eye** (ERES structured) — secure, lingerie-derived
- **Razorback / T-back** (Matteau, Skims) — distributes load across shoulders
- **Convertible multi-way** (Matteau, Monday) — user chooses configuration
- **Single-strap / asymmetrical** (Oséree, contemporary) — architectural statement

**Load Distribution Principle:** Wider back straps and multi-point attachment (razorback) reduce pressure points. ERES uses wider elasticated back bands on structured styles.

### 5. Waistline & Leg Openings

| Approach | Brands | Construction |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| **High-cut / French cut** | ERES, Hunza G | Elongates leg line; requires precise elastic tension |
| **Moderate / classic** | Solid & Striped, Monday | Broadest fit tolerance |
| **High-waist / retro** | Monday, Matteau | Panelled construction for smoothing |
| **Cheeky / Brazilian** | Oséree, contemporary | Minimal back coverage; precise curve grading |

**Technical Detail:** Leg opening elastic must be 1:0.85 to 1:0.9 stretch ratio (elastic shorter than opening) for secure fit without cutting in. Hunza G's crinkle fabric self-gathers at openings.

### 6. Fabric Texture, Stretch & Recovery

**Material Categories:**

| Category | Brands | Composition | Key Properties |
|----------|--------|-------------|----------------|
| **Luxury matte nylon** | ERES, Matteau | PA 78% / EA 22% | High recovery, chlorine-resistant, matte hand |
| **Crinkle textured knit** | Hunza G | PA/EA textured | 4-way stretch, forgiving fit, hides texture |
| **Seamless sculpt** | Skims | PA/EA seamless knit | Compression zones, second-skin feel |
| **Metallic lurex** | Oséree | PA/EA + metallic yarn | Reflective, slightly stiffer hand |
| **Recycled ECONYL** | Skims, many contemporary | 100% regenerated PA | Identical performance to virgin nylon |

**ECONYL Verified Properties (from technical sources):**
- 78% polyamide / 22% elastane typical for swim
- Chlorine resistance: >100 hours (ISO 105-B02)
- UV protection: UPF 50+ achievable
- Recovery: >95% after 50% extension
- Identical dye uptake to virgin nylon

### 7. Seams, Lining & Finishing

**Seam Technologies:**
- **Traditional stitch + serge** — most brands, lined styles
- **Ultrasonic bonding** — ISAORA, ERES (select styles), emerging in luxury
- **Bonded + taped** — high-end technical; eliminates bulk
- **Raw-edge bonded** — seamless aesthetic; requires precise tension

**Lining Strategies:**
- **Full lining** (ERES, Matteau) — powernet or self-fabric; doubles fabric cost
- **Front lining only** (many contemporary) — cost/weight savings
- **No lining / self-lined** (Skims, Hunza G crinkle) — fabric dense enough
- **Powernet panels** (strategic) — targeted support without full lining

**Finishing Details:**
- **Coverstitch hems** — flat, stretchable, professional
- **Folded elastic** — clean interior, visible topstitch
- **Raw-edge bonded** — minimal, architectural
- **Hardware:** 316L stainless or brass (chlorine-resistant); plastic sliders for adjustability

### 8. Hardware & Functional Details

| Hardware Type | Brands Using | Material | Notes |
|--------------|-------------|----------|-------|
| **Slide adjusters** | Monday, Solid & Striped | Plastic (acetal) | Chlorine-resistant; matte finish |
| **Hook-and-eye** | ERES | Coated metal | Lingerie-grade; rust-resistant |
| **Rings/slides** | Matteau (convertible) | 316L stainless | Hidden when strapless |
| **Chain** | Oséree | Brass/gold-plate | Decorative-functional hybrid |
| **None (bonded)** | ISAORA, ERES select | N/A | Most architectural |

**Key Insight:** The most "architectural" brands **minimise visible hardware** — Matteau hides convertible hardware; ERES bonded styles eliminate it entirely; ISAORA uses zero hardware.

### 9. Fit Inclusivity & Size Adaptability

**Approaches Observed:**
- **Monday Swimwear:** "Monday Body" — 30+ sizes (XS-4X, multiple cup fits per band); adjustable everything; fit quiz
- **Hunza G:** Crinkle fabric stretches 2-3 sizes; single SKU covers range
- **Skims:** Compression grading; 9 shades × 10 sizes; shapewear logic
- **ERES:** Traditional luxury grading (FR 36-44); structured fit requires precise sizing
- **Matteau:** XS-XL; convertible straps accommodate proportion variance

**Verified Detail:** Monday's fit guide separates band and cup sizing (e.g., 34DD vs 36D) — rare in swimwear. Hunza G's fabric stretches ~150% horizontally.

### 10. Product Photography & Campaign Art Direction

**Visual Language Patterns:**

| Brand | Lighting | Environment | Composition | Model Direction |
|-------|----------|-------------|-------------|-----------------|
| ERES | Studio + natural | Architectural pools, stone | Clean, centred, negative space | Confident, direct gaze |
| Hunza G | Bright natural | Beach, water, colorful tiles | Playful, movement, texture close-ups | Natural, joyful |
| Matteau | Soft natural | Minimalist architecture, sand | Geometric, architectural framing | Serene, architectural |
| Monday | Bright studio + lifestyle | Diverse locations | Inclusive casting, real bodies | Relatable, confident |
| Skims | Controlled studio | Seamless cyclorama | Product-focused, sculptural | Diverse, body-neutral |

**Campaign Consistency Principles:**
1. **Each collection has a distinct palette/environment** (not just garment changes)
2. **Lighting serves the material** — matte needs soft; metallic needs contrast
3. **Casting reflects customer** — age, ethnicity, body type diversity
4. **Negative space is intentional** — not empty, but compositional
5. **Detail shots serve construction story** — strap attachment, seam, hardware

## Technical Principles for SOLUN Development

### From Research — Non-Negotiable Construction Standards

1. **Elastic tension ratios:** Leg/waist openings 1:0.85–0.90; necklines 1:0.90–0.95
2. **Recovery requirement:** >95% after 50% stretch (ECONYL meets this)
3. **Chlorine resistance:** Minimum 50 hours; target 100+ hours
4. **UPF:** Target 50+ for all fabrics
5. **Hardware:** 316L stainless or chlorine-resistant plastic only
6. **Seam allowance:** 6mm for bonded; 10mm for stitched + serged
7. **Lining:** Powernet (80g) for support zones; self-fabric for coverage

### Architectural Design Principles (Derived from Matteau + ERES + ISAORA)

1. **Seam as structure** — every visible seam carries load or shapes garment
2. **Hardware elimination** — internal architecture replaces external components
3. **Convertible geometry** — single garment, multiple wearing modes
4. **Material honesty** — fabric properties dictate form, not decoration
5. **Proportional harmony** — neckline, strap, leg opening relate geometrically
